Welcome to Big Fish Bistro

You want fish?

The Big Fish Bistro started as a nightmare, what if you could only eat fish? So we decided to make that nightmare a reality. Now at the Big Fish Bistro, you can't ever escape the smell and taste of fish. In our first couple months of opperation, no one arrived to try our food. That was no surprise to us, this place is a nightmare, but for some reason a random social media influencer decided to review us, and now we have 3 locations and a non-insignifigant portion of the fish and chips market in our name. Thanks to you, we can now all fish.
